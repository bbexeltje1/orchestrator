CHANGE IN v1.1.0
----------------
Added: one-touch "just play" experience - no separate terminals.
  1. play.cmd: double-click launcher. Runs start.ps1 with an execution
     policy bypass, keeps the window open on failure, forwards arguments
     (e.g. -Force for an unattended first run).
  2. Background service auto-start: start.ps1 now starts Ollama
     ("<detected ollama> serve") and any service with a configured
     services.*_start_command (Qwen/llama.cpp, OmniRoute, Arena bridge)
     as hidden background processes, waits for a real health answer and
     reports the result. Services that are already running are only
     probed and never touched. Startup output is captured in
     logs/service-<name>.out.log and .err.log; PIDs are recorded in
     run/<name>.json.
  3. Interactive console: the launcher now ends in
     "python -m orchestrator.cli console" - a driftline> prompt
     (help/doctor/report/status/submit/approve/init/quit) that routes
     through the exact same argparse handlers as the non-interactive
     CLI. Everything is driven from the one window.
  4. stop.ps1 now also stops exactly the background services the
     launcher recorded (process-tree stop), and nothing else.
  5. New config section "services" (auto_start, health_wait_seconds,
     ollama/qwen/omniroute/arena start commands) in config.py,
     config.yaml and config.example.yaml, with validation.
Fixed: offline tests no longer write data/orchestrator.db outside their
  own tmp dirs (conftest anchors config.storage.database to the test
  base dir).

Earlier: v1.0.1 fixed .\start.ps1 -Doctor on a fresh extraction
($Script:VenvPython used before Initialize-VirtualEnv ran).
